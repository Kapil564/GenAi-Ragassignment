📥

Document Ingestion

Upload PDF or plain text files up to 20 MB. The system extracts text from every page automatically.

✂️

Recursive Chunking

Text is split using RecursiveCharacterTextSplitter — preserving paragraphs & sentence boundaries with 200-char overlap.

🧬

Google Embeddings

Each chunk is embedded with text-embedding-004 — Google's high-quality embedding model.

🗄️

Qdrant Vector DB

Embeddings are indexed in Qdrant for blazing-fast similarity search with cosine distance.

🔎

Semantic Retrieval

Your question is embedded and matched against the top-5 most relevant chunks from the document.

🤖

Grounded Generation

Gemini 2.0 Flash generates answers strictly from the retrieved context — not from its general knowledge.
